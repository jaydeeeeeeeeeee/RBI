How to use this template

1
Click the link above → on GitHub, click "Use this template" → "Create a new repository"
2
Clone your new repo and set up XAMPP + MySQL. Import projectrbi.sql to create the database.
3
Open config.php and update your barangay name, number, and database credentials.
4
Replace images/brgy410_logo.png with your own barangay logo. You're all set!
